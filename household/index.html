The Dias Family Mission

Through our fear of the Lord, by the power of the Holy Spirit, we fully submit to God’s plan for marriage. This is lived out in the following ways:

  1. To symbolize Christ’s love and pursuit of His Church through our marriage.
  2. To teach and demonstrate the Word of God to our children.
  3. To extend hospitality to our neighbors and those in need.
  4. To wisely invest the time and resources entrusted to us.
