Statement of Faith and Purpose

By walking with God and being filled with the Holy Spirit each day, I, Caleb Dias, seek to fulfill my purpose in life: to know and make known the glory of God and His Son Jesus Christ—the way of salvation—in the following ways:

  1. To love and seek God with all of my heart, soul, mind, and strength.
  2. To faithfully proclaim the grace and truth of God in spite of trial, persecution, and great tribulation.
  3. To love, serve, and guard my household, with whom I have been entrusted.
  4. To disciple fellow brothers in Christ and honor those who have gone before me.
  5. To aspire to the highest level of dedication and craftsmanship in my work.
  6. To submit to God-given authority and serve others with justice, mercy, and humility.

By the strength and mercy of Jesus Christ, to whom I have entrusted my soul, I abandon all pursuits of wealth, power, and titles of men, returning all glory to God who sits in Heaven.
