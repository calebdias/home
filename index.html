Statement of Faith and Purpose

I, Caleb Stephen Dias, publicly confess that I am a great sinner saved by the Lord Jesus Christ. By walking with God and being filled with the Holy Spirit each day, I seek to fulfill my purpose in life: to know and make known the glory of God and His Son Jesus Christ—my savior—in the following ways:

  1. To love and seek God with all of my heart, soul, mind, and strength.
  2. To faithfully proclaim the grace and truth of God in spite of trial, persecution, and great tribulation.
  3. To love, serve, and guard my household, with whom I have been entrusted.
  4. To disciple fellow brothers in Christ and honor those who have gone before me.
  5. To aspire to the highest level of dedication and craftsmanship in my work.
  6. To submit to God-given authority and serve others with justice, mercy, and humility.

The Dias Family Mission

Through our fear of the Lord, by the power of the Holy Spirit, we fully submit to God’s plan for marriage. This is lived out in the following ways:

  1. To symbolize Christ’s love and pursuit of His Church through our marriage.
  2. To teach and demonstrate the Word of God to our children.
  3. To extend hospitality to our neighbors and those in need.
  4. To wisely invest the time and resources entrusted to us.

By the strength and mercy of Jesus Christ, to whom I have entrusted my soul, I abandon all pursuits of wealth, power, and titles of men, returning all glory to God who sits in Heaven.
