Purpose

Through prayer, meditation, the careful study of Scripture, fasting, wise counsel, and hard work, by the power of the Holy Spirit, I seek to fulfill my purpose in life: to know and make known the glory of God and His Son Jesus Christ—the way of salvation—in the following ways:

  1. To fear God, obey His Word, and walk with Him.
  2. To love, guide, protect, and provide for my household, with whom I have been entrusted.
  3. To proclaim the grace and truth of God through all platforms I am afforded.
  4. To honor my father, mother, and those who have gone before me.
  5. To serve the Church—the Body of Christ.
  6. To submit to God-given authority and lead with justice, mercy, and humility.
  7. To extend charitable acts of service and giving.
  8. To remain faithful in spite of trial, persecution, and great tribulation.

By the strength and mercy of Jesus Christ, to whom I have entrusted my soul, I abandon all pursuits of wealth, power, and titles of men, returning all glory to God who sits in Heaven.
